Shorts against long form

Kept apart because they are not comparable: a Short is measured on swipes, an episode on watch time. The median matters more than the mean here, since one clip that happens to get picked up drags the mean above anything the channel will repeat.

Everything uploaded, everywhere

Built from what the platforms report as well as from the register, so a video published since the last edit of the register still appears here on the next collection run. A row marked NOT IN REGISTER went out without being written down.

Everything made, and where it went

One row per asset, one column per platform. A cell is green only when there is a URL proving it, because a half-finished post is the dangerous case: it feels done and is not. A dash means that platform never carries that kind of asset, which is different from missing.

What to post next

Built from the empty cells, spread across days at each platform's own pace. TikTok takes one clip a day: three in five minutes from a young account reads as spam to its early distribution and they get throttled together.
